Death and Life Are in the Power of the Tongue | sermon | Apostle Sir DeAndre Knight
============================================================

[Full sermon manuscript or detailed notes. Recorded on camera; the outline above
drives the on-screen point titles and scripture overlays.]

1. Words are seeds  (Proverbs 18:20)
   What you say, you eat. The fruit of the mouth feeds the belly.
2. The Word created, the Word sustains  (Genesis 1:3; John 1:1)
   God framed the world by speaking. We are made in His image.
3. The Bible as God's trust and estate  (Isaiah 40:8)
   Grantor, instructions, beneficiaries for all generations.
4. Choose life with your lips  (Proverbs 18:21; Colossians 3:16)
   Practical: 7-day speech fast from complaining.
