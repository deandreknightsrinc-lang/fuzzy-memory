KNIGHT LYFE ULTIMATE - INSTALLING ON A MAC
===========================================

EASY WAY (recommended) - one command does everything
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
2. Type  bash  and a space. Then drag the file "install.sh" from this folder
   into the Terminal window (that types its location for you). Press Return.

   If this folder is in Downloads you can instead paste exactly:

       bash ~/Downloads/Knight-Lyfe-Ultimate-macOS/install.sh

3. Type your Mac password when asked (nothing shows while you type - that is
   normal) and press Return.

The installer unzips the plug-ins, puts the Logic plug-in, the VST3 and the
app where they belong, removes macOS's "downloaded from the internet" block,
signs them for your Mac and checks that Logic will accept the Audio Unit.
Skip to step 5.

BY HAND (if you prefer)
-----------------------
Unzip the files first (double-click each .zip).

1. LOGIC PRO PLUG-IN (Audio Unit)
   Copy "Knight Lyfe Ultimate.component" to:
       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (In Finder: Go > Go to Folder..., paste the path, press Return.)

2. STANDALONE APP (optional)
   Drag "Knight Lyfe Ultimate.app" into Applications.

3. VST3 (optional, for other DAWs)
   Copy "Knight Lyfe Ultimate.vst3" to:
       /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3

4. LET MACOS OPEN IT
   These builds are not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS will block them at
   first. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and paste these lines
   (each starts with sudo; copy them exactly, including the quotes):

       sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Knight Lyfe Ultimate.component"
       sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Knight Lyfe Ultimate.app"
       killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar

   Type your Mac password when asked (nothing shows while you type).

   "No such file or directory" means the file isn't in that folder yet - do
   steps 1 and 2 first. For the app you can also right-click it > Open > Open,
   or go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".

5. USE IT IN LOGIC
   Restart Logic Pro. Logic checks new plug-ins at start-up; if it says the
   plug-in failed validation, open Logic Pro > Settings > Plug-in Manager,
   select Knight Lyfe Ultimate and click "Reset & Rescan Selection".

   New track > Software Instrument > Instrument slot >
   AU Instruments > Knight Lyfe > Knight Lyfe Ultimate

   Play your keyboard: you hear the grand piano through Logic, and the
   keyboard, chords, score and learn mode follow along.

   TEMPO SYNC: with "Follow Logic" ticked (on the Groove panel), pressing
   Play in Logic starts the loaded song and the drum groove in time with
   Logic's tempo and bar position, and they stop when Logic stops.

   Songs, Audio -> MIDI, grooves, the Kit Rack and everything else from
   Knight Keys are inside.

6. THE STANDALONE APP
   Click "Options" (top-left) > Audio/MIDI Settings to choose your audio
   output and turn on your MIDI keyboard and electronic drum kit.

7. YOUR KEYBOARD AND ELECTRONIC DRUMS
   Both are "class compliant": plug them into the Mac with USB, no driver
   needed. In Logic they work right away on the Knight Lyfe track. In the
   standalone app tick them in Options > Audio/MIDI Settings.

   AKAI MPK MINI PLUS (keyboard)
   - Plug in the USB-C cable. The keys play the grand piano, light up the
     keyboard and score, and drive Learn mode (Songs > Right hand / Left
     hand / Both hands).
   - The 8 pads play the drums (they send drum notes on channel 10).

   ALESIS NITRO MAX (drum kit)
   - Connect the module's USB port to the Mac with the USB cable from the
     box. It sends on channel 10, which Knight Lyfe plays as drums.
   - Hit the kit: the pads, the Kit Rack and the drum highway light up.
     Rim shots, hi-hat and cymbal edges and the hi-hat pedal are all
     understood.
   - To learn a song: Songs > Drums on any song. Open the Drums panel and
     click Highway: the notes fall toward the line, a white box shows what
     to hit, and the song waits for you.
   - If you hear the module's own sounds as well, turn the module's volume
     down and listen through the Mac (or your Quantum interface).

   KIT RACK (Drums panel > Kit Rack)
   - Pick a kit on the left, click a drum to hear it, turn Tune / Decay /
     Level. Drop your own WAV, AIFF or MP3 onto a drum to make your own kit.
   - Your kit is saved in ~/Library/Application Support/Knight Lyfe/Kit and
     plays in every Logic project, even with the plug-in window closed.

8. STAGE (THE GAME)
   Click "Stage" at the top. Choose who's playing (rename players by
   double-clicking), the instrument and a song:
   - Perform: play along in real time. Every note is scored Perfect,
     Great, Good or Miss; keep a streak for x2, x3, x4 points; earn up to
     5 crowns. Each song has a family leaderboard.
   - Practice: the song waits for you at every note.
   One crown on a song unlocks the next one. If your hits always land a
   bit late, raise "Timing" (in ms) in the Stage menu.

9. CHURCH SONGS, HARDER LEVELS AND YOUR OWN SONGS
   Click "Songs":
   - Church & Worship: hymns and spirituals, plus worship and gospel chord
     progressions to practice for service.
   - Arrangement: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced (harmony, walking
     bass, gospel voicings, busier drums). Stage has the same levels.
   - "+ New song": type any song's chord chart (and a melody if you
     want) and save it to My Songs. "Save the open song" keeps a MIDI file.
     Your songs show up in Songs and in Stage > My Songs.

10. YOUR OWN SOUND LIBRARY (folder "Sound Library Tools")
   In Terminal:  bash  (drag in setup-library.sh)  and press Return.
   Pick your external drive. It builds an organized "Knight Lyfe Sound
   Library" (Drums, Keys, Bass, Synths, Choir & Vocals, FX, Loops, MIDI...)
   and links it into Logic: Library > User Patches > Knight Lyfe, the
   Sampler instrument menu, channel strip settings and project templates.
   Drop new sounds into its Inbox folder and run organize-samples.sh to sort
   them by name. See README.md in that folder.
   FREE SOUND PACKS: the setup also installs the Knight Lyfe Sound Packs
   from "Sound Library Tools/Sound Packs": Knight Lyfe Drums Vol 1 (kicks,
   snares, claps, hats, cymbals, toms, percussion, 808s in every key) and
   Knight Lyfe Gospel MIDI (drum grooves and chord progressions in every
   key). Look for the pack's name inside Drums/Kicks, Drums/808s, MIDI/...
   If your Downloads are on another drive, drag setup-library.sh from the
   download into Terminal instead of typing the path.

11. MULTI-OUTPUT DRUMS (each drum on its own mixer channel)
   - New Software Instrument track > Instrument slot > AU Instruments >
     Knight Lyfe > Knight Lyfe Ultimate > Multi-Output (16xStereo).
   - On the track's channel strip in the Mixer, click the small "+" under
     the meter once for each drum output. Logic adds aux channels.
     Out of the box the drums go by type:
       outputs 3-4 Kick, 5-6 Snare, 7-8 Hi-Hat, 9-10 Toms,
       11-12 Cymbals, 13-14 Percussion.  1-2 is the main mix (piano + rest).
   - CHOOSE YOUR OWN: open the Kit Rack (Kit button) and use "Logic outputs"
     at the bottom. Pick an output for any drum (15 drum outputs, up to
     channels 31-32), or press "Each drum on its own" to give all 15 drums
     their own channel, "By drum type" to go back, or "All on main".
     The line under the list says how far to click "+" in Logic.
   - Now put EQ, compression and reverb on each drum separately.
   - If a drum goes quiet, its aux channel hasn't been created yet: click
     "+" again until you reach its output (or pick the Stereo version).
   - Drums on their own outputs skip the plug-in's built-in Room reverb;
     use Logic's effects on those channels instead.
   - The routing is kept with your Kit Rack, so every project gets it.
     A project made with the older Multi-Output (7xStereo) version: pick
     Multi-Output (16xStereo) again on that track.

12. LESSONS (PIANO PATH)
   Click "Lessons" at the top: a step-by-step piano course for each
   player, from finding middle C to church chords and both hands. Play
   the yellow keys; earn stars; the next lesson opens when you pass; come
   back daily to grow your practice streak.
   On the website you can also press "Microphone" to play a keyboard that
   has no USB (single notes; headphones help).

13. SINGING: VOICE COURSE AND VOCAL BOOTH
   Lessons > "Voice" tab: singing lessons with the microphone (find your
   range and choir part, match notes, scales, church songs, four-part
   "Amen", and vocal production in Logic: Flex Pitch, Pitch Correction,
   Melodyne, Auto-Tune, AVOX, OVox, ACE Studio).
   "Vocal" at the top opens the Vocal Booth: live pitch line and tuner,
   and "Make choir parts" for the open song (soprano, alto, tenor, bass).
   "Choir MIDI" saves four named tracks: drag the file into Logic (one
   track per part) or import it into ACE Studio and type the lyrics.
   Singing needs the microphone, so use the website (Safari or Chrome) for
   the singing parts; choir parts and MIDI export work in the plug-in too.
   The choir sound plays on the website; inside Logic use Logic's own
   choir patches or your choir plug-in on the exported tracks.

14. SHEET MUSIC: FULL SCORE, SCORE -> MIDI AND READING LESSONS
   "Score" at the top shows the open song as sheet music, one staff per
   instrument. Untick parts to print just one player's part. "Print / PDF"
   prints (choose "Save as PDF" in the print window; on the website).
   "MusicXML" saves the score for Logic (File > Import > MusicXML),
   MuseScore or ACE Studio. "MIDI" saves the song as a MIDI file.
   Score -> MIDI: "Open score" (or Open...) takes a MusicXML file
   (.musicxml / .mxl) and plays it; "Save as MIDI" keeps it as MIDI.
   Printed or PDF music: drop it in the AI Studio's inbox/sheet folder on
   kl-oracle (or use Audiveris on the Mac); you get a .mxl and a .mid back.
   Lessons > "Read music": learn the staff, both clefs, sharps and flats,
   key signatures and rhythm, then read songs with no lit keys.

15. DRUM LESSONS
   Lessons > "Drums": a step-by-step drum course for the Alesis Nitro Max
   (or the pads on screen): the parts of the kit, first beats, crashes and
   fills, and church grooves (hymn 3/4, gospel shuffle, two-step). Each
   beat: first it waits for every hit, then you play it in time with a
   click and get a timing score.

16. GUITAR AND BASS LESSONS
   Lessons > "Guitar" or "Bass" (on the website, with the microphone on):
   tune every string, learn the notes and first chords on a fretboard
   picture, then strum (or play roots on bass) along with a click to
   Worship Flow and Amazing Grace. It hears single notes and strummed
   chords. Headphones help, so the speakers don't play for you.

17. BAND ROOM (REHEARSE AS A BAND OR CHOIR)
   "Band" at the top: pick a song and press Start rehearsal. Every part
   (lead vocal, keys, guitar, bass, organ, drums, and the choir's soprano,
   alto, tenor, bass) is played by an AI musician until you set it to one
   of you; then the AI drops out of that part. "Choir rehearsal" does the
   same for the choir sections: mark who is here, the AI choir sings the
   rest. Works with 1 person or the whole family.
   In Logic, the plug-in plays piano and drums only: use "Band MIDI" and
   drag it into Logic to give each part its own Logic instrument.

18. LYRICS (SING ALONG)
   "Lyrics" at the top shows the words of the open song, lighting up as
   they're sung. Built in for the hymns and starter songs; for your own
   songs, type the words in the Song Builder (Songs > New song). Karaoke
   MIDI files (.kar) work too. The words also appear in the Band Room,
   on the printed score, and in MIDI files you save.

19. COURSE STUDIO AND AI TEACHERS
   Lessons > "Course Studio": make a course for any subject (lessons with
   talks, videos, quizzes and music exercises), taught by a Knight Lyfe
   character. "Video script" writes what the teacher says for each lesson;
   make the video in HeyGen, Synthesia or another AI video tool, then paste
   its link (or pick the file) to add it to the lesson. Save puts the
   course in Lessons; "Course pack" saves it as one file to share.
   In the Band Room, each missing position shows the character covering it.

20. VIRTUAL CHURCH (WEBSITE)
   "Church" at the top: plan the order of service (your church's name,
   colors, links), then "Run the service". Worship songs play with the AI
   band and the words on screen. "Open stream screen" opens a clean window:
   put it on the projector, or capture it in OBS (free) to go live on
   YouTube or Facebook. Arrow keys move through the service.

21. ASK THE TEACHER (AI ON KL-ORACLE)
   1. Run the AI Studio setup again on kl-oracle (it adds HTTPS for the AI).
   2. On the Mac, once: open http://<kl-oracle-ip>:8089, download root.crt,
      double-click it, set it to "Always Trust" in Keychain Access.
   3. Open https://<kl-oracle-ip>:8443 in Safari or Chrome (Knight Keys with
      the AI ready), or in the website/plug-in press Ask > ⚙ and enter that
      address. In a lesson press "💬 Ask" and talk to your teacher.

22. PUPPET STUDIO (PLAY A CHARACTER LIVE, WEBSITE)
   In Church (or Ask the teacher) press "🎭 Puppet". Pick the character,
   turn on "📷 Camera" and the character copies your face (head, eyes,
   eyebrows, smile, mouth); "🎤 Voice" moves the mouth when you talk. Look
   straight at the camera and press "🎯 Center". Hold keys 1-4 for
   expressions (happy, surprised, wink, thinking); 5-8 pop a wave, praise
   hands, music notes or a heart. "📺 Open puppet screen" opens the
   character on a green screen: in OBS add a Window Capture of it with a
   Chroma Key filter, then go live. Face tracking runs on the Mac (no video
   leaves it) and needs Safari or Chrome on the website; the plug-in has
   the keys only.

23. CHURCH PLATFORM (BAC MINISTRIES AND OTHER CHURCHES, WEBSITE)
   The website's front page leads to BAC Ministries' church home: online
   church, free music lessons with the AI teachers, band and choir
   rehearsal, kids and giving. To fill in BAC's details, open "Free church
   platform", press "Edit BAC Ministries", fill in the form, download
   church.json and replace church/churches/bac-ministries.json. Another
   church uses the same form ("New church") to get its own page, powered
   by BAC Ministries.

24. THE BIBLE (READ, LISTEN, WATCH)
   On the church page press "Bible". Pick a version (King James with the
   Apocrypha, Berean Standard Bible in modern English, and more), a book and
   chapter, or type a reference like John 3:16. "Listen" reads it with a
   voice cast; "Watch" shows it full screen. Enoch, Jubilees and Jasher
   are on their own shelves, labeled.
   To make the real audio Bible and videos, on kl-oracle run the AI Studio
   setup again, then for example:  kl-bible make kjv John 3 --vertical
   In the church setup, put https://<kl-oracle-ip>:8443/bible-media under
   "Audio and video Bible server", and the Bible page plays them.
