# K.I.S.S. Hyphy Beats: Keep It Simple, Stupid
*W.O.M.P. · Knight Lyfe. Twenty simple Bay Area hyphy beats built on one rule:*

> **A jingle, an 808, a snap or a clap. That's the beat.**
> At most one extra sound. If a beat needs a fifth element, it isn't K.I.S.S.

These are **original** ice-cream-truck-style jingles: short, catchy, childlike melodies that turn menacing over a heavy
808. They don't copy any real truck tune or song. Use them as their own beats, or flip any album song onto one of them.

---

## How to Read Each Beat
- **Jingle:** two bars of 8th notes, looped. `-` means hold the previous note. Octaves use scientific names (C4 = middle C).
- **808 grid:** 16 steps per bar, in groups of 4. `x` is an 808 hit, `-` is rest. Steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 are beats 1, 2, 3 and 4.
- **Snap/clap grid:** the same 16 steps. Hyphy lives on 2 and 4 (`---- x--- ---- x---`).
- **808 root:** the 808's home note and frequency. Tune the 808 to it.

## The K.I.S.S. Kit in Logic (one template for all 20)
| Track | Build it | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| **JINGLE** | Retro Synth. *Ice-cream chime:* sine plus a little triangle, amp attack 0, decay 350 ms, sustain 0, release 250 ms, a light 5.5 Hz vibrato. *Toy synth:* a square wave instead. *Music box and glockenspiel:* use a Sampler patch if you have one. | **WOMP Dust** makes the "truck speaker" sound: Low Cut 300 Hz, High Cut 5 kHz, Wow 25%, Flutter 15%, Crackle 0%, Mix 100%. The jingle should sound like it's coming out of a warped PA horn. |
| **808** | Sampler 808 or Retro Synth sine | **WOMP 808 Glide**: Mode Retrigger + Slide, Glide Time 40–60 ms. **WOMP Low End**: Drive 55%, Sub Boost 3 dB, Mono Below 120 Hz. The 808 *is* the kick, so skip a separate kick (or use a very short one). |
| **SNAP / CLAP** | Drum Machine Designer: finger snap plus a hand clap layered | **WOMP Pocket**: Swing % 54, Snare/Clap Lay-back 4 ms, Velocity Humanize 6. Short room reverb only, no big plates. |
| **EXTRA** (optional) | One ear-candy sound per beat (listed below) | Keep it low in the mix and don't repeat it every bar |

**Mix rule:** the 808 and the clap are the loudest things; the jingle sits just under them. No hi-hats (or one open hat on the and of 4 at most).
**Master:** −8 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.

## The K.I.S.S. Suno Formula
Style = **K.I.S.S. core** + the beat's line + (optional) a vocal line.

**K.I.S.S. core (paste first):**
```
hyphy Bay Area hip hop, minimalist bouncy West Coast beat, simple catchy ice cream truck jingle melody, heavy booming 808 bass, simple finger snap and hand clap, sparse drums, lots of space, keep it simple
```
**Exclude Styles (all K.I.S.S. beats):**
```
orchestral, strings, choir, busy drums, trap hi-hat rolls, EDM, rock, pop, lo-fi, jazz, complex chords
```
**Optional vocal line (add at the end):**
- STALEMATE: `gritty baritone male rap, energetic hyphy flow, chanted hook`
- HAZEL: `gritty male rap verses, soulful female hook, smooth male R&B croon`
- Beat only: `instrumental`

**Settings:** Weirdness 30–35% and Style Influence 75–80%, which keeps it minimal. If Suno adds strings, choir or busy
drums, move "minimalist" and "sparse drums" to the front.

## Flipping an Album Song onto a K.I.S.S. Beat
1. Pick the beat whose "Fits" line matches the song, or any beat in the song's key.
2. In the song's Suno prompt, replace everything after the core and flavor blocks with the K.I.S.S. beat's line.
   Or use the K.I.S.S. core on its own plus the vocal line.
3. Keep the lyrics as they are. Hyphy at 96–108 BPM sits right in the Bounce lane, so the flows already fit.
