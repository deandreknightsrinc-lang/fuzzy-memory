    Design a compound in five steps
    1. Click an estate on the map.
    2. Click a template on the left to drop a compound on it.
    3. Click a building to change it, drag it to move it.
    4. Fix anything listed under Site rules.
    5. Press Walk to explore, then Save and export to keep an option.
