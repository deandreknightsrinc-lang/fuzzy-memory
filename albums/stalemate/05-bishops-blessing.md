# 05. Bishop's Blessing
**Album:** STALEMATE by King Bishop Knight · **Chess concept:** The bishop's diagonals: it never comes at you straight, only at an angle

## Story Concept
Sunday, 11 a.m., at Diagonal Ave Baptist. Mama Knight sits in pew three in her white hat, squeezing the hand of her seventeen-year-old son in a borrowed suit, and she believes the prayers are working. Deacon Bishop preaches the loaves and the fishes, how a little becomes a lot, and Knight smirks because that is exactly how the re-up works. After the benediction, down in the fellowship-hall basement behind the choir robes, Bishop lays hands on the duffel bags, prays over the product, and takes his ten percent tithe, never once looking anybody in the eye. On the way out the side door, Knight sees the Deacon by the dumpster whispering into a flip phone while a gray sedan idles at the curb and pulls away the moment Knight looks. He shrugs it off. It is a small detail the album will cash in on "Discovered Attack," and the turn here is Knight realizing his mother's church and his boss's business share a building, a pastor, and a collection plate.
