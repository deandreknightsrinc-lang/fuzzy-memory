# 02. Pawn Shop
**Album:** STALEMATE by King Bishop Knight · **Chess concept:** Pawns: the smallest pieces, always first to be traded away

## Story Concept
January, and Knight is thirteen. A pink FINAL NOTICE from the electric company is taped to the apartment door, Mama is at the kitchen table punching the same numbers into a calculator, and Tre is doing homework by the open oven for heat. While Grandma dozes over her Bible, Knight takes her gold cross off the dresser and walks eight snowy blocks to Sal's Main Line Pawn, where a glass case holds the whole neighborhood's history: a choir trumpet, a wedding band, a Purple Heart. Sal gives him sixty dollars and a ninety-day ticket, the lights come back on, and Grandma touches her bare neck and only says, "I know, baby. And the Lord know too." Outside the shop, Fats, one of King Reese's lookouts, has clocked the whole thing and plants the first seed: you ain't gotta sell your grandma, come see us. The turn is Knight learning that everyone in the 64 is somebody's pawn, and that the ticket runs on a clock too.

## W.O.M.P. Production Notes (Logic Pro)
- **BPM / Key / Swing:** 90 BPM, G minor (808 root G1, about 49 Hz). 16th swing at 57%.
- **Progression:** Verses: Gm9 → C9 (i9 → IV9). Hook: Gm9 → F → Eb → D7#9. V3 (Grandma's "I know, baby"): Cm9 → D7#9 → Gm9. Outro resolves plagal-minor, Cm9 → Gm9.
- **Drums:** Dusty kit, snare plus rimshot. Kick on 1, the "a" of 2 and 3; snare on 2 and 4; open hat on the "and" of 4. WOMP Pocket: swing 57%, snare +12 ms. Snow-crunch foley under the V1 walk.
- **Bass:** 808 roots G1/C1, 70 ms glide up to Bb at each phrase end. Synth bass (ES2 Moog-style) walks 8ths up high. V2 bars 1–4: 808 out, synth bass walks the full range. WOMP Low End drive 25%.
- **Samples & Keys:** Replay a 70s-style female wail in Sampler, −3 semitones, through WOMP Dust. Vintage EP "Wurli" (tremolo 30%) with WOMP Soul Chords min9/min11. Door bell and register ding as one-shots. Whistle doubles the hook an octave up at −12 dB.
- **Street Gospel layer:** Grandma's Bible, not a service: a slow-Leslie B3 and a 3-voice choir hum only under V3. No tambourine; it's January and the house is quiet.
- **Arrangement map:** Intro 4 (door bell, clock, Sal's line) → V1 16 (drop drums bars 15–16 so the ding lands on the hook downbeat) → Hook 8 (whistle double enters) → V2 16 (808 out bars 1–4) → Hook 4 → V3 8 (organ and hum in) → Outro 4 (spoken, register ding, piece click).
- **Vocal direction:** Conversational, telling it on the stoop. Sal gruff and dry. Punch-in doubles on "holy thing" and "clock in the hood."
- **Mix note:** Dip the vocal chop 2–3 kHz whenever the rap is on. High-pass the wind at 400 Hz and sit it at −24 dB.
- **Suno tips:** Knight Persona, Weirdness 40%, Style Influence 70%. If it drifts to jazzy lo-fi, push Style Influence to 75% and regenerate; keep the take where the chop and the hook melody lock.
