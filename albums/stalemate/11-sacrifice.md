# 11. Sacrifice
**Album:** STALEMATE by King Bishop Knight · **Chess concept:** Sacrifice: giving up a piece on purpose to save a more valuable one

## Story Concept
A gray Monday in April, ninth floor of the county courthouse, two months after the raid. Lil Tre, sixteen and charged as an adult, stands up in an orange jumpsuit and swears the backpack from fourteen was his and his alone, so the weight never reaches his big brother. Knight, who pleads down to eleven months in county on a lesser charge, sits two rows back in a suit bought with promotion money while Mama Knight grips the pew with Grandma's gold cross in her fist. The judge gives Tre four years state, and Tre winks at Knight as the bailiff takes his elbow. It is the emotional center of the album: the promotion was never free, because the pawn was paying for it the whole time.

## W.O.M.P. Production Notes (Logic Pro)
- **BPM / Key / Swing:** 70 BPM half-time, D minor (808 root D1, about 36.7 Hz). 16th swing at 58%, the deepest drag on the album.
- **Progression:** Verses: Dm9 → G9 (i9 → IV9, slow). Hook: Dm9 → C → Bb → A7#9. Bridge (piano only): Bbmaj7 → Am7 → Gm7 → A7. The final hook ends on the Amen G → Gm → D: the one major chord is Tre's wink.
- **Drums:** Soft kick, dry crisp snare on beat 3 (half-time), sparse 8th hats. WOMP Pocket: swing 58%, snare +20 ms. At the V2 gavel: everything cuts for 1 beat, the clock stops, then the drums return without it.
- **Bass:** Bass Player (fingerstyle) carries the song. 808 only swells under the hooks, sustained with a 120 ms glide. V2 bars 1–4 (the laundry-room memory): 808 out as usual, bass alone. WOMP Low End drive 20%.
- **Samples & Keys:** Replay a 70s soul "ooh" cry in Sampler, pitched −2 semitones, through WOMP Dust with heavy wow. Studio Strings legato, LPF 8 kHz. Soft piano plus Vintage EP with WOMP Soul Chords min9. Clock and gavel in Quick Sampler.
- **Street Gospel layer:** A low choir under every hook, slow-Leslie B3 underneath. No tambourine or claps; nobody is celebrating.
- **Arrangement map:** Intro 4 (clock, spoken) → V1 16 (drop drums bars 15–16) → Hook 8 → V2 16 (808 out bars 1–4; gavel on bar 10, clock stops) → Hook 8 → Bridge 8 (piano only, Tre's letter) → Hook 6 → Outro 4 (clock resumes, piece click).
- **Vocal direction:** Restrained and close, almost no ad-libs. Punch-in doubles only on "Tre's dime." Tre's letter: a younger male voice, dry and close, a touch of tape.
- **Mix note:** Master at −11 LUFS (ballad). Sidechain the strings 2 dB from the lead vocal so the story stays on top.
- **Suno tips:** Weirdness 30%, Style Influence 75%. If it turns into a piano-pop ballad, regenerate; keep the take where the drums still hit hard at 70.
