# STALEMATE by King Bishop Knight
*A 20-track chess-themed storytelling hip-hop album. First draft. See `../PROJECT-BIBLE.md` for the world and characters.*

| # | Title | Chess concept | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Opening Move | The opening | A chess clock starts ticking. Knight introduces the 64: born a pawn on a board he didn't build. |
| 02 | Pawn Shop | Pawns | At 13, Knight pawns Grandma's gold cross to keep the lights on. Everyone in the 64 is somebody's pawn. |
| 03 | L-Shaped Life | How the knight moves | Why Knight never moves straight: school, then a detour, then the corner. The only piece that jumps over others. |
| 04 | King of the 64 | The king | The legend of King Reese: untouchable, rarely moves, and everyone dies protecting him. Knight's first meeting. |
| 05 | Bishop's Blessing | The bishop's diagonals | Sunday service at Diagonal Ave Baptist. Deacon Bishop blesses the congregation, then the product. Organ-heavy. |
| 06 | Castle | Rooks and castling | Holding down the Rooks, the tower stash houses. Castling means swapping places to protect the King. Crew anthem. |
| 07 | Promotion | A pawn promoted to queen | The pawn reaches the last rank: first real money, first car, Mama's rent paid. It feels like winning. |
| 08 | Queen's Gambit | Sacrificing to gain | Knight meets Hazel and gives up something to win her. Bridge track into the HAZEL album. |
| 09 | En Passant | Capturing in passing | His day-one friend Smoke robs him in passing, a betrayal done on the move. Knight's first real decision. |
| 10 | Check | Check | The first raid. The King is attacked and everyone has to respond. |
| 11 | Sacrifice | Sacrificing a piece | Lil Tre takes the charge for Knight. The pawn given up to save the Knight. Emotional center. |
| 12 | Fork | The knight fork | One move threatens two pieces: King Reese wants retaliation, Hazel wants him out. Torn in two. |
| 13 | Pinned | The pin | Agent Cole pins him: move and the King is exposed. Interrogation-room story told in real time. |
| 14 | Touch Move | Touching a piece commits you | The night he touched the gun. No take-backs. A shooting and its aftermath. |
| 15 | Discovered Attack | Discovered attack | The truth comes out: Deacon Bishop was the informant all along. The church scene, revisited. |
| 16 | Zugzwang | Every move makes it worse | Forced to move, and every move hurts: snitch, fight, run, or sit. |
| 17 | The Clock | Time control | Mama Knight's voice and the ticking clock. Time runs out for everybody: Mama's health, Tre's sentence, Knight's youth. |
| 18 | Checkmate | Checkmate | King Reese falls. The empire ends in one move. Funeral and fallout. |
| 19 | Stalemate | Stalemate | Title track. Alive and not in check, but no legal moves. Not free, not dead. Nobody wins. |
| 20 | Reset the Board | Setting up a new game | Outro. The pieces go back in the box. A letter to his unborn daughter: learn the board, don't become a piece. |
