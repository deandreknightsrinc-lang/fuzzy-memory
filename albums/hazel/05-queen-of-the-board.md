# 05. Queen of the Board
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Hard hip-hop anthem

## Story Concept
Fourth of July at the foot of the Rooks: King Reese's cookout, with smoke off the grills, gold rope, white linen, and the whole crew eating in his shadow. Knight lays out the board (pawns up front, Bishop on the angles, the King who never moves) and explains why the Queen outranks all of them. King Reese calls him over and tells him to trade the girl, because a queen is a liability and a tell. Before Knight can answer, Hazel walks up with a plate, looks the King dead in the eye, and tells him his blood pressure is too high and he should go easy on the salt and the shade. The cookout goes silent until Reese laughs and raises his cup, and the whole 64 learns what Knight already knew. This is the crossover with STALEMATE's "Queen's Gambit," the moment Hazel becomes a piece everyone on the board has to account for.
