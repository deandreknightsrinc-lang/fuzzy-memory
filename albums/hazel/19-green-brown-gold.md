# 19. Green, Brown, Gold
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** none · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Bounce · 100 BPM · D-flat major

## Story Concept
December, months after the Friday on 9th. Hazel drives herself to the testing center on Route 9 for her nursing boards, sits in the parking lot until the sun hits the rearview, and sees her eyes go green for the first time since the shooting. The song walks through her whole motif, one color per verse: green is the morning she takes back; brown is the windowless exam room, where question forty-one describes a woman with a gunshot wound to the left flank and Hazel laughs out loud, because she answered that one from the gurney, and where the screen goes black at question eighty-five; gold is two days later in Mother Ruth's kitchen, when the license lookup says RN and she cries for herself for the first time in her life. At 2 AM she circles a warehouse job listing in green ink and sends the text that STALEMATE's title track receives at the snowy chess table: "Come home. Need to talk." The turn is her terms, which answer "no legal moves": a stalemate ends the game, so stand up. The Knight leaves the board, or the Queen does, and she already knows how to walk.
