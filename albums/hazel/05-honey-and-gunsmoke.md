# 05. Honey & Gunsmoke
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** Knight · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Knock · 146 BPM · F minor

## Story Concept
A Friday in August at Lucille's on the east side of the 64, the wrong side of Diagonal Ave for a girl from Mother Ruth's porch: fried whiting in the grease, hot honey in a jar, and a burgundy '86 Cutlass outside that Knight didn't own a month ago (she does the math). The air conditioning is cold as a morgue, so he puts his jacket on her shoulders, and under the cologne in the lining she smells burnt matches: gunpowder, three days old. She sets down her fork and runs a nurse's intake interview in rhyme (chief complaint, allergies, surgical history, pain scale) with two fingers on his wrist across the table. Knight answers straight: Tuesday night a Regal shot at the Rooks, he fired four times at its back window, and he has been reading the obituaries first ever since. His pulse stays at seventy-two. The turn is her decision, made like a dosage: she'll take him in small amounts, titrate slow, and wash the jacket in Ruth's lye soap. The honey comes out clean and the smoke never does, a quiet warning for "Crossfire."
