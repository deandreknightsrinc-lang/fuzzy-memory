# 01. The Queen's Side (Intro)
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** Rook · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Bounce · 96 BPM · Eb minor

## Story Concept
Night, years after everything, in a dark room with one Rhodes chord hanging in the air. Rook sits close to the mic and sets the board the way the streets always tell it: King, Knight, Bishop, the pawn who paid. Then he turns the chair around, because every story about the 64 was written from the King's side and nobody ever studied the Queen. Hazel's first sung line comes out of the dark: they wrote her in the margin, in pencil, in case a king needed the room. When the 808 drops she introduces herself like a nurse reading a chart: name, block, mother, father, the job at Pop's, the eyes. She plants every thread the album will pull: the green ink, the ledger with the lipstick blot, three bullets in a Cutlass ashtray, a barbershop dream on Ninth, Mercy General, and a room numbered 214. Rook closes by admitting he owes her something he'll only explain at the end, which is the first hint that he stood between her and the second shot.

## Voice Map
- **Hazel:** sung 16 lines / rap 16 lines (50% sung). Out of the dark, a low, unhurried alto that sits almost on the Rhodes, a gospel lift only on "the Queen's side." Then a clipped, clinical rap that gets faster as the list gets heavier.
- **Guests:** Rook: 10 lines (spoken). He sets the board, turns the chair, and makes the promise that pays off in the last song.
- **Hazel share of all lines:** 76%
