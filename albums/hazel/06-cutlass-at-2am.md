# 06. Cutlass at 2AM
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Late-night R&B

## Story Concept
Two in the morning in late spring, the '86 Cutlass parked behind the old high school under the water tower, rain on the windshield and the quiet storm on the radio. Hazel has come straight from clinicals with her feet on the dash, smelling like hospital soap and cocoa butter. She lays out her plan: the RN pin, Mother Ruth crying in the front row, eleven hundred dollars in a coffee can, and a house with a yard where the sirens can't reach. When she asks Knight for his dream, he admits the only thing he ever planned at twelve was his own funeral, then says it out loud for the first time: a barbershop on Ninth with chess boards in the window to teach the young boys to think. She writes it in the margin of her notes next to the valves of the heart, which makes it real, and they talk until sunrise turns her eyes green. It's the tenderest scene on the album and the dream the rest of the record fights to protect.
