# 18. Green, Brown, Gold
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Soul R&B

## Story Concept
Day three in the ICU at Mercy General. Knight has been sleeping in the vinyl recliner by the bed, watching the monitor more than the TV, when Hazel finally opens her eyes at dusk. The song is built around the motif of her eyes: brown in the dark of the room when she first wakes and doesn't know where she is, green when he opens the blinds and the morning light hits her face, and gold when she sees him and starts to cry. Each color becomes a verse and a memory: brown for the Cutlass nights and the arguments, green for the corner store where he first saw her, gold for every time she cried because of him. The turn is that this time the gold is not from pain, it's from relief, and Knight promises that the next tears she cries will be happy ones.
