# 16. Cold Side of the Bed
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** none · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Bounce · 94 BPM · E-flat minor

## Story Concept
A few months after the county gate, in Hazel's one-bedroom on her side of Diagonal. Knight is free but never home: Reese has him running something he won't name, and he comes in at dawn smelling like outside. It's 11:40 PM after a sixteen-hour double at Mercy General; she eats Sunday's cold rice standing at the sink, flips NCLEX flashcards at 3 AM (she knows the antidote for everything but him), and calls Mother Ruth, who tells her about the husband who kept her on the porch for forty years. Then one Sunday he comes in with dryer lint on his collar from a laundromat they don't use and a burnt smell she files away without asking, and after that he doesn't come home at all: five nights in the Cutlass, texting "I'm safe." She texts "Come home"; he texts "Not yet. Not like that." The turn is her last move: "Then pick me up tomorrow. 12:45. Be on time. One time." He writes back, "I'll be early." That's the Friday of "Crossfire," and STALEMATE's "Touch Move" seen from the other side of the bed.
