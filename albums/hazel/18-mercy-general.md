# 18. Mercy General
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** Mother Ruth, Knight · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Bounce · 98 BPM · G major

## Story Concept
Thirty days at Mercy General, the hospital where Hazel trained, told from inside the bed. On day three the tube comes out, and the first thing she does is read her own chart upside down off a classmate's clipboard: exploratory laparotomy, splenectomy, four units, GSW left flank. She lost her spleen and kept her name, and she's funny about it, because that's how nurses survive. Her whiteboard says "Goal: walk to the door" in a classmate's handwriting. Mother Ruth brings her choir, still in their robes, into the hallway; by week three Hazel is on the step-down unit with a walker and a gait belt, and the TV tells her what the 64 is doing without her: King Reese taken down at a fish fry, helicopters Monday, twenty-two names, and Deacon Bishop gone, undone by the decimals she circled. Every night the night nurse says Knight is in the chapel again. The turn is hers: after thirty days of everyone praying for her, she takes her cane and her IV pole down one floor, kneels beside him, and prays for him. On 3-West, an old man with no visitors raises his hand as she passes.
