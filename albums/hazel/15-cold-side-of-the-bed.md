# 15. Cold Side of the Bed
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Female-led R&B (Hazel's point of view)

## Story Concept
Hazel's side of the story, a few months after Knight came home. He's free, but he isn't home: King Reese wants retaliation for the robbery and the raid, and Knight is gone every night until the sky turns gray. Hazel comes off a double shift at Mercy General at 11:40 PM, eats cold rice standing at the counter, and lies awake reaching for a side of the bed that stays cold until dawn. She calls Mother Ruth, who tells her about the grandfather who kept her waiting the same way, and Hazel realizes she has been more alone with him out than she was with him locked up. The turn comes when Knight finally slips in at 5 AM and she pretends to be asleep, and for the first time she lets herself think about whether she'll still be there when he's ready to stay.
