# 17. Pray for Hazel
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Gospel soul rap

## Story Concept
4:30 AM in the chapel on the second floor of Mercy General, six hours into Hazel's surgery. Knight, still in the blood-stiff shirt from the night before, kneels in a room with eight pews and a stained-glass window lit by a vending machine, trying to pray to a God he stopped trusting the day he watched Deacon Bishop bless the product. Mama Knight arrives with a thermos and a coat, and then Mother Ruth walks in with her church choir, still in their robes from an all-night vigil, and begins to sing without saying a word to him. Knight bargains: the corner, the money, the Cutlass, his own freedom, anything for Hazel. The turn comes when Mother Ruth finally takes his hands, not to forgive him but to pray with him, and a nurse opens the chapel door to say Hazel is out of surgery.
