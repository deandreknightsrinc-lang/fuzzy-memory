# 04. Diagonal Ave
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Smooth hip-hop

## Story Concept
Golden hour on Diagonal Ave, the avenue that cuts the 64 in half: the Rooks, the corners, and the ice-cream truck to the east; steeples, starched Sunday whites, and nosy porch lights to the west. Every evening at seven Knight crosses with his hands out of his pockets so the curtains can see he's empty, and he walks past a Deacon Bishop whose smile lasts a little too long. Hazel answers from her side: the church ladies at the beauty shop gossip, and she's the choir girl with anatomy flashcards who has already buried her own trouble. They meet on the painted yellow line at the bus stop while the traffic curses them from both shoulders. By the end the line stops being a border and becomes their spot, and Hazel reminds him that a Knight moves in L's but a Queen moves any way she wants.
