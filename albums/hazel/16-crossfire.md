# 16. Crossfire
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Hardcore, cinematic

## Story Concept
The worst night of both albums, the fallout from STALEMATE's "Touch Move." It's a humid Friday, 12:50 AM, and Knight is picking Hazel up from her shift in the '86 Cutlass so she won't wait at the bus stop, the one time she asked him to be on time and he was. At the light on Diagonal Ave and 9th, a gray Impala rolls up on the passenger side, the retaliation meant for Knight, and Hazel is hit in the side. Knight drives her to Mercy General, the hospital where she works, running every light with one hand pressing her wound and her coworkers running out to meet them. The song ends not with revenge but with Knight standing alone under the ER lights, soaked in her blood and understanding that every move he ever made led to this square.
