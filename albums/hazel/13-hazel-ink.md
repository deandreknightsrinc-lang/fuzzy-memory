# 13. Hazel Ink
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Soul, boom-bap

## Story Concept
Months into the bid, Knight and Hazel keep each other alive through letters, and she only writes in green ink because it's the color her eyes turn in the sun. The verses alternate: Knight writing from a top bunk by the light of the corridor, then Hazel writing at Mother Ruth's kitchen table after a clinical shift, then Knight again. Through the mail we learn things neither could say on the visiting-room phone: Lil Tre got moved upstate, Hazel almost quit school, Knight is learning to read the chessboard differently. The turn arrives in the last letter, when Knight admits he's been sniffing the pages to find her perfume and Hazel sends back one blank page with a single green line: "Come home straight."
