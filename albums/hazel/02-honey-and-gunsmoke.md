# 02. Honey & Gunsmoke
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** R&B/rap

## Story Concept
Their first real date, a Friday night on the east side of the 64, the wrong side of Diagonal Ave for a girl from Mother Ruth's porch. Knight picks Hazel up in the freshly waxed '86 Cutlass and takes her to Lucille's for fried whiting with hot honey, then to the Skate-A-Rama for a slow skate. In the parking lot at 11:40, a tinted Regal creeps by and opens fire, and a fifteen-year-old named Dre gets hit in the shoulder. Knight reaches for his waistband, but Hazel grabs his wrist, takes his jacket, and holds pressure on the wound until the ambulance comes, blood to her elbows on a yellow sundress. It's the night Knight learns she's stronger than him, and the first time the street's violence touches her.
