# 01. Hazel (Intro)
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Soul croon into rap

## Story Concept
July in the 64, a late-80s summer afternoon at Pop's Market on the corner of Ninth and Diagonal Ave. Seventeen-year-old Knight, pager on his hip and a knot in his sock, walks in for a Pepsi and finds Hazel Monroe on the register with a nursing textbook open next to the scratch-offs. The sun cuts through the window and her eyes go green, and when he tries to overpay she slides the money back: she knows exactly who he is and won't take tips from the corner. He waits by the payphone until she clocks out, sees her eyes turn brown under the streetlight, and walks her to the 6:15 bus. The album opens on a crush that is already a warning, and it closes on a quiet prayer that he'll never be the reason her eyes turn gold.
