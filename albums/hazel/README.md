# HAZEL by Hazel (narrated by Rook)
*Rewrite v2. Hazel Monroe's own album: she **sings about 60%** and **raps about 40%** of her lines. Guests help the story, and
**Rook** narrates. Produced in W.O.M.P. Uptempo 808 Mode (`../../production/WOMP-SOUND-GUIDE.md`). World and canon:
`../PROJECT-BIBLE.md`.*

**Motif:** "Green in the sun, brown in the dark, gold when she cry."
**Tags:** each lyric section header names the performer and the mode, e.g. `[Chorus - Hazel (sung)]` or `[Verse 2 - Hazel (rap)]`.
