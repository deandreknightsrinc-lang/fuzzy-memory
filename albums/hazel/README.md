# HAZEL
*A 20-track storytelling album in R&B soul, hip-hop, and hardcore rap. First draft. Hazel is the Queen from STALEMATE. See `../PROJECT-BIBLE.md`.*

**Motif:** "Green in the sun, brown in the dark, gold when she cry."

| # | Title | Mode | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hazel (Intro) | Soul croon into rap | Knight sees her for the first time at a corner-store register, in the sun. The eyes. |
| 02 | Honey & Gunsmoke | R&B/rap | First date on the wrong side of town: sweetness and danger in the same breath. |
| 03 | Mother Ruth's Porch | Soul, gospel | Hazel's grandmother interrogates Knight on the front porch. Sweet tea and a warning. |
| 04 | Diagonal Ave | Smooth hip-hop | Their neighborhoods are split by the avenue. Love across the line. |
| 05 | Queen of the Board | Hard hip-hop anthem | Knight explains why she's the most powerful piece. Ties to STALEMATE. |
| 06 | Cutlass at 2AM | Late-night R&B | Parked in the '86 Cutlass, talking till sunrise about dreams: nursing school, getting out. |
| 07 | Ride, Don't Die | Hardcore rap and soul hook | She'll ride for him, and he fights to keep her out of it. |
| 08 | Her Father's Pistol | Hardcore rap | Hazel's father, an ex-hustler, warns Knight at gunpoint. Knight sees his own future. |
| 09 | Lipstick on the Ledger | Dark R&B | Hazel secretly starts keeping his books. Love turns into complicity. |
| 10 | Voicemail (Interlude) | Spoken soul | A voicemail from Hazel on the night of the raid (STALEMATE "Check"). |
| 11 | Glass House | Aggressive R&B/rap duet | Jealousy, another girl's text, a thrown phone. Both of them throw stones. |
| 12 | Visiting Hours | Soul rap | Knight is locked up on a short bid. Plexiglass, a phone receiver, her palm against his. |
| 13 | Hazel Ink | Soul, boom-bap | Prison letters written in green ink. Verses alternate between his letters and hers. |
| 14 | She Held It Down | Hardcore anthem | A tribute to the woman who held everything down while he was gone. |
| 15 | Cold Side of the Bed | Female-led R&B | Hazel's point of view: nights alone, double shifts, and holding it together. |
| 16 | Crossfire | Hardcore, cinematic | A retaliation shot meant for Knight hits Hazel. The worst night. |
| 17 | Pray for Hazel | Gospel soul rap | Knight in the hospital chapel, bargaining with God. Mother Ruth leads the choir. |
| 18 | Green, Brown, Gold | Soul R&B | She wakes up. A song built on the color motif: every mood of her eyes. |
| 19 | Still Hazel | Grown R&B/hip-hop | After the storm: scars, therapy, forgiveness, choosing each other again. |
| 20 | Forever Hazel (Outro) | Wedding soul into rap | A courthouse wedding and a baby girl on the way. The Queen leaves the board, and so does the Knight. |
