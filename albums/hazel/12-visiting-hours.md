# 12. Visiting Hours
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Soul rap

## Story Concept
Saturday, 10 AM, county lockup off Route 9, a few months into Knight's short bid. Hazel took two buses in her yellow Sunday dress, got patted down, and sat on a bolted stool with thirty minutes on the clock. Through scratched plexiglass and a greasy phone receiver she tells him she passed her pharmacology exam and that Mama Knight's lights are back on, while he lies about how easy it is inside. The song moves the album from the chaos of the raid into the long separation, and the turn is the final minute: the guard calls time, they press their palms to the glass, and Knight realizes this is the first time he has ever been the one left watching somebody walk away.
