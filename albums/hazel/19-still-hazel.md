# 19. Still Hazel
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Grown R&B/hip-hop

## Story Concept
Eight months after the shooting, on a Tuesday evening, in the small apartment they rented far off the 64, past where the bus line ends. Hazel has a scar along her ribs she won't wear crop tops over, a therapist named Dr. Okafor she sees every Tuesday at four, and a startle every time a car backfires; Knight has a warehouse job, a probation officer, and a phone he doesn't answer when the old numbers call. King Reese is gone, Bishop is exposed, Tre still has two years, and Knight is living in the stalemate: no legal moves back, no clean way forward. The song is about the unglamorous work of staying: couples counseling, apologizing without excuses, her learning to be angry out loud and him learning to hear it. The turn comes when Hazel, standing in front of the mirror, lets Knight trace the scar and says that she's changed, but she's still Hazel, and she's still choosing him.
