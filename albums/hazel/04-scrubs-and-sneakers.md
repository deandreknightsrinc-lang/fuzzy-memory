# 04. Scrubs & Sneakers
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** none · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Knock · 144 BPM · C minor

## Story Concept
The grind, then a Thursday in July when the air conditioner at Pop's Market (Ninth and Diagonal) dies and the sun comes straight through the glass. Hazel's day starts at 5:40: the 7:10 bus to clinicals at Mercy General, saltines and the Krebs cycle on a napkin for lunch, a bus-bathroom change into the Pop's smock, and the register till midnight at three thirty-five an hour, doing the math on what's left for Mother Ruth's light bill. For three weeks she has kept a chart on a boy in a white tee who passes at 3:40 every day, never sells in front of the store, and holds the door for Ms. Dot. So when he finally walks in for a Pepsi and stares at her eyes going green in the sun, she's the one with the file: she says his name before he says it, reads his pager, the knot in his sock and the ankle he favors, and slides his twenty back ("I don't take tips from the corner, Knight"). The turn is that the album's famous meeting (which Knight tells in STALEMATE as the day he found her) was her diagnosis first. He waits eight hours at the payphone for a no, and the waiting is what gets her.
