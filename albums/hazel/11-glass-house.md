# 11. Glass House
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Aggressive R&B/rap duet

## Story Concept
3:12 AM in the one-bedroom over the laundromat on Hazel's side of Diagonal Ave, weeks after the raid. Knight's phone lights up on the nightstand with a text from a girl saved as "Keys," and Hazel reads it before he's awake. The phone hits the wall, the screen spiderwebs, and when Knight fires back he pulls up the med-school tutor who's been walking Hazel to her car after night class. Both of them throw stones, both of them are guilty of something, and the argument escalates verse by verse. The turn comes at the end: barefoot, both of them kneel to pick up the broken glass and cut their hands on the same shard, and the fight dies in the quiet of bandaging each other.
