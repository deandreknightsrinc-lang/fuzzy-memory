# 11. Glass House
**Artist:** Hazel · **Featuring:** Knight · **Narrator:** Rook · **Lane:** Knock · 148 BPM · Bm

## Story Concept
3:12 a.m., three weeks after the raid, in the one-bedroom over the laundromat on my side of Diagonal Ave. Knight is out on bond with an April court date and Tre is still in a cell. His phone lights up on the nightstand: "Keys," a heart, a key, and "still got your hoodie." I read it three times like a chart, and then I throw it overhand, the way Daddy threw lug nuts in the tire yard, and the screen spiderwebs against the plaster. Knight fires back with Marcus, the tutor with the flashcards who walks me to my car on Thursdays and carried my book bag, the one with the ledger in it. The turn is that I admit my own stone. I didn't kiss Marcus, but I let myself picture a life with no sirens in it, and I've lied to Grandmama for twenty-one nights running. We kneel barefoot to sweep the glass, cut our fingers on the same shard, and I tape us both up with butterfly strips from under the sink.

## Voice Map
- **Hazel:** sung 30 lines / rap 18 lines (62.5% sung). V1 is furious double-time with punch-ins, and V3 is slower and ashamed, half-time. The chorus is a gospel-trained alto belt, and the bridge is soft, narrated, almost lullaby.
- **Guests:** Knight — 8 lines — his counter-accusation rap. Rook — 4 lines — spoken intro and verdict.
- **Hazel share of all lines:** 80.0%
