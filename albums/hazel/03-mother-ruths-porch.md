# 03. Mother Ruth's Porch
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Soul, gospel

## Story Concept
A Sunday evening a week after the shooting, on the west side of Diagonal Ave. Knight is summoned to Mother Ruth's front porch: crepe myrtle, a red porch swing, a funeral fan from Diagonal Ave Baptist with Deacon Bishop's face printed on it, sweet tea poured strong, and a shotgun leaning inside the screen door. Ruth interrogates him about his mother, his "little this and little that," and his buzzing pager, then tells him how Hazel's mother Lorraine lost herself to the pipe and how the only time Ruth ever saw the girl's eyes turn gold was at Lorraine's funeral. Hazel defends him from the doorway, and Ruth admits she already saw the bloody jacket. Knight gets grace and a warning in the same glass, plus an order to be at church at ten, seated on the left, because Ruth doesn't sit on Deacon Bishop's side.
