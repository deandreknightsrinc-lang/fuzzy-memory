# 07. Ride, Don't Die
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Hardcore rap and soul hook

## Story Concept
October, with police heat on the 64 like a fever. King Reese needs a spare tire full of product driven from the Rooks to a pickup by the river, and the police are stopping every car with a young man driving alone. Hazel, in scrubs on Knight's couch, hears the call and lays out the logic like a nurse reading a chart: nobody pulls over a couple with a nursing student riding along. Knight snatches her keys, drops her at Mother Ruth's, and makes the run alone through a checkpoint where an officer taps the loaded tire with his flashlight and waves him on only because a wreck comes over the radio. When Knight gets back at 4 a.m. she's on the porch with Ruth's Bible and a shawl, and for the first time he sees her eyes go gold, and he knows he caused it. They make a deal (she rides by standing, he doesn't die), and both of them know he's lying.
