# 08. Her Father's Pistol
**Album:** HAZEL · **Mode:** Hardcore rap

## Story Concept
Monroe Tire & Brake, behind Seventh Street by the tracks. Hazel's father, Big Otis Monroe, is two years out of a twelve-year bid and now patches flats for Deacon Bishop's church vans, and he calls Knight into the back office. A .38 sits on the desk beside a photo of nine-year-old Hazel in white lace. As a tire man, Otis can tell from how the Cutlass's spare rides low exactly what Knight is carrying. Otis raps his own story: he ran for the King before Reese, his product took Lorraine, he missed her funeral from upstate, and Hazel's green-ink letters stopped when she turned fifteen. He presses three bullets into Knight's palm (one for the man coming for him, one for Knight, one for whoever is standing next to him) and tells him the street will kill him for free, he only wanted Knight to meet the man he's going to become. Knight walks out with a glimpse of his future and tells Hazel his first lie to her face.
