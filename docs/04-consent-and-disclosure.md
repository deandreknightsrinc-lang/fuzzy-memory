# Likeness Consent and AI Disclosure

AI copies of real ministers' faces and voices only work if people trust them. This page keeps BAC Ministries, and every ministry you white-label for, on solid ground.

## 1. Consent before cloning

Don't make a voice clone or avatar of anyone until they have signed a consent. After they sign, set `consent_on_file: true` for them in the brand profile. The service builder refuses to build any AI segment for someone without it.

### Sample likeness consent (have a lawyer adapt it for your state)

> I, ______________________, authorize **[Ministry Name]** and its production provider **[Your Company]** to create and use a digital replica of my voice and/or image ("Digital Likeness") for the following purposes only: weekly worship service videos, ministry announcements, the ministry's publication and related media.
>
> 1. **Approval.** No content using my Digital Likeness will be published without being based on a script I (or the Senior Pastor, if I so designate) have approved.
> 2. **Limits.** My Digital Likeness will not be used for political endorsements, commercial endorsements outside the ministry, or any statement I have not approved.
> 3. **Labeling.** Content using my Digital Likeness will be labeled as AI-assisted where platforms require it.
> 4. **Revocation.** I may revoke this consent in writing at any time. Within 30 days the voice and avatar models will be deleted and no new content will be produced. Content already published before revocation may remain unless I request removal.
> 5. **Ownership.** My Digital Likeness belongs to me. It may not be sold, transferred, or used for any other client.
>
> Signature: ____________________  Date: __________
> Ministry representative: ____________________  Date: __________

## 2. Telling viewers

- **YouTube:** when uploading, answer **Yes** to "Altered or synthetic content" whenever a realistic AI voice or presenter appears. Not doing this can get videos labeled or removed.
- **Description line** (written automatically into `youtube.md`): "Portions of this service were produced with AI tools, with the permission of the ministers shown. The sermon is delivered by the pastor."
- **To members:** one short announcement when you launch, explaining what's AI and what isn't. Congregations handle this well when they hear it from the pastor first. Finding out on their own feels like a betrayal.

## 3. Copyright checklist

- **Music:** CCLI Streaming License for covers, or original / royalty-free music only.
- **Scripture versions:** KJV is public domain in the US. NKJV, NIV, NLT, and ESV let you quote a limited number of verses with credit (see each publisher's permissions page). Add the version abbreviation after each reference.
- **Articles and research:** summarize in your own words and credit the source (for example "Source: MIT News"). Don't paste whole articles.
- **Images:** use your own photos, AI-generated images, or licensed stock. Don't use screenshots from other people's YouTube videos.
