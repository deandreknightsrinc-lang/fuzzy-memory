        🎵 Starter songs

          Built-in songs are public-domain melodies with Knight Keys arrangements. Songs still under copyright (most modern worship songs) can't be included, but you can type their chord charts into ＋ New song for your own practice.

          More hymns: hymnary.org has free public-domain hymn MIDI files for thousands of hymns (look for the MIDI link on a hymn tune's page). Download one, open it with Open…, then press 💾 Save the open song. Four-part hymns play soprano + alto in the right hand and tenor + bass in the left, and single-track files can be split at middle C when you save them.

          Audio → MIDI

          Runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded. Solo piano works best. On full songs it catches the strongest parts, so expect to clean up. Sensitivity and shortest note update the result instantly after converting.

          Song Builder

          Bars go between | lines, and chords in one bar share it. Without bar lines, each chord is one bar. G:2 gives exact beats, % repeats the last chord, N.C. is no chord, x2 at the end repeats a line, and labels like Verse: or [Chorus] are fine. Chords like Cmaj7 Dm7 G7sus4 Gadd9 D/F# Bbm7b5 all work.

          Melody: a note name and octave (middle C is C4), then :beats if it's not one beat. r:1 is a rest, and F#4 and Bb3 are sharps and flats.

          📜 Full Score

          Open a song (Songs, Open…, or Audio → MIDI) to see its full score.

          🎼 Choir parts (SATB)

          Four-part harmony for the open song: soprano sings the melody, alto, tenor and bass get the chord under every note, hymn style. Learn your part, or export it for ACE Studio, a choir plug-in or Logic.

          Pitch tools: Flex Pitch, Melodyne, Auto-Tune, ACE Studio, OVox, AVOX
          • Logic Flex Pitch (built in, like Melodyne): Cmd-F, set the track to Flex Pitch, double-click the region and drag the note blobs.
          • Logic Pitch Correction (built in, like Auto-Tune): set Root and Scale to the song's key; slow Response sounds natural, 0 is the robot effect.
          • Melodyne (Celemony) opens right in Logic's track (ARA). Auto-Tune (Antares) for real-time tuning; AVOX (Antares) for harmonies, doubles and choir from one voice.
          • OVox (Waves): vocoder, talkbox and synth harmonies that follow the chords you play.
          • ACE Studio: import the choir MIDI, type the lyrics, pick a voice and it sings each part; bring the audio back into Logic.
          • The Voice course in 🎓 Lessons walks through each one. These are commercial apps from their makers, not part of Knight Keys.
