# Studio Road Case Desk

An interactive, single-page template for an L-shaped studio desk built from two flight cases:

- **Tower Case** at the corner holds the computer (vented door, rear exhaust, side cubby).
- **Keys Case** at the end of the main run carries rack gear and storage.
- Torsion-box **desktops** span the cases; the keys wing rests on the Tower Case and a leg.

Open `index.html` in a browser (or the GitHub Pages site under `/studio-desk/`). Every size follows your settings: desk height, run lengths and depths, computer size, keyboard, rack space, screen or projector, material and road-case options. Settings are saved in the browser.

## What it gives you

- Drawings: overview, floor plan, both case fronts, desktop build-up and road mode.
- Fit checks: computer clearance, leg room, keyboard, rack width, doorway, top pack, leather roll.
- Cut list with layer counts for laminated cardboard (single, double or triple wall) or plywood.
- Faux-leather pieces with wrap allowance, yardage packed onto your roll width, and adhesive.
- Shopping list: leather, adhesive, aluminum angle, ball corners, latches, handles, casters, knobs.
- Desk ↔ road case: the Tower lid becomes the leg, the Keys lid becomes a footrest, tops split into a strapped top pack.
- Step-by-step build: laminating with wheat paste or PVA, torsion boxes, cases, lids, caster boards, hardware backers, leather wrapping.

Measurements are a starting point; measure your own gear and room before cutting.
