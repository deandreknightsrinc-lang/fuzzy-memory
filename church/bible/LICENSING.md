# Modern Bible versions: asking permission

Modern translations (NKJV, NIV, ESV, NLT, AMP, NASB, CSB, ...) belong to their
publishers. Publishers usually let churches quote a limited number of verses with a
copyright notice, but putting a **whole Bible** in an app, and especially
**recording it as an audio Bible or making videos of it**, needs a written license.
Audio rights are often handled separately from print rights (some translations
already have an exclusive audio Bible, the way the NKJV has *The Word of Promise*).
Check each publisher's current permissions page for their exact rules before
you write.

| Version | Publisher (permissions department) |
|---|---|
| New King James Version (NKJV) | HarperCollins Christian Publishing (Thomas Nelson) |
| New International Version (NIV) | Biblica (Zondervan in the United States) |
| English Standard Version (ESV) | Crossway |
| New Living Translation (NLT) | Tyndale House Publishers |
| Amplified Bible (AMP), New American Standard Bible (NASB) | The Lockman Foundation |
| Christian Standard Bible (CSB) | Holman Bible Publishers |

Until a license arrives, the app uses its public-domain versions, including the
Berean Standard Bible for modern English.

## Letter template

Fill in the brackets and send it to the publisher's permissions department.

> **Subject:** Permission request: [VERSION] in a free church Bible app, with dramatized audio
>
> Dear Permissions Team,
>
> I am writing on behalf of BAC Ministries, [city, state], to ask permission to use
> the [VERSION] in a free Bible app for churches.
>
> **Who we are.** BAC Ministries is a [church / ministry] that provides a free online
> church platform for small churches: online services, music lessons, and a Bible.
> Nothing is sold; there are no ads and no fees for churches or listeners.
>
> **What we would like to do.**
> 1. Display the full text of the [VERSION] in the app and on our churches' web pages,
>    with your copyright notice on every page.
> 2. Produce a dramatized audio recording of the [VERSION] text (a narrator and
>    character voices, with background music), word for word, for free listening
>    in the app.
> 3. Produce videos of the readings (scene pictures with the recorded audio and the
>    text as subtitles) for the app and for our churches' YouTube and social media
>    channels.
>
> The text would never be altered, abridged or paraphrased. [Optional: The voices are
> generated by AI, and each recording is checked word for word against the text.]
>
> **Reach.** About [number] churches and [number] listeners in the first year.
>
> We are glad to follow your attribution requirements, report usage, and send you the
> recordings for review before release. Please let us know whether this is possible,
> what terms or fees apply, and whether audio rights are available.
>
> Thank you for your work in making God's Word available.
>
> Sincerely,
> [Name], [Title]
> BAC Ministries
> [email] · [phone] · [website]
